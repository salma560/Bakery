Our Story

Baked with Love, Served with Pride

For over three decades, Family Bakery has been a cornerstone of the Los Angeles community. What started as a small neighborhood oven has grown into one of the city's most beloved artisan bakeries.

Every loaf is shaped by hand, every pastry perfected through generations of family recipes. We source only the finest organic flours and local ingredients to bring you authentic flavor in every bite.
